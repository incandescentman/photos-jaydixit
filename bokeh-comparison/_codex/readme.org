#+title: How to use the bokeh comparison gallery

Open [[file:/Users/jay/Dropbox/github/photos-jaydixit/bokeh-comparison/_codex/index.html][the local gallery]] directly in your browser. No server or installation is needed.

- Choose a comparison, then press Space or use Show A / Show B.
- Choose Hide lens names for randomized blind A/B ordering. Reveal preserves your current image and ordering.
- Use Side by side, scroll to zoom, and drag to pan. Zoom and pan stay synchronized.
- Use 100% pixels for one downloaded-image pixel per physical display pixel. Review exports cannot reveal unavailable original-camera detail.
- Entire frame resets zoom and alignment. Published composite extracts instead offer Entire extracted view; Open original shows the preserved composite.

See [[file:/Users/jay/Dropbox/github/photos-jaydixit/bokeh-comparison/_codex/sources.org][sources and limitations]] for verification and comparison conditions. Original JPEGs remain unchanged. The [[file:/Users/jay/Dropbox/github/photos-jaydixit/bokeh-comparison/_codex/manifest.json][manifest]] records paths, source URLs, hashes, and comparison membership for later pooling.
