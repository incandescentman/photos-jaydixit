#+title: Bokeh Comparison Sources and Verification

* Scope
Personal photographic research only. Downloads are retained unchanged, with source SHA-256 hashes. No publication or redistribution. Photographs only; no video frames. Camera resolution and review-export resolution are distinguished in each record. A 100% view means one downloaded source pixel per display pixel; it cannot restore missing camera detail.

* Evidence gaps
No qualifying photographic same-scene bokeh comparison located for Sigma 85mm f/1.2 versus DG DN f/1.4, or Sigma 85mm f/1.2 versus Sony GM II. Abbott's Sigma f/1.2 cross-lens panels are sharpness chart comparisons, not background-rendering comparisons.

No usable SongRaw-versus-Sigma matched-aperture photograph pair found. Video-only evidence was deliberately excluded.

Five GM II/original GM scene pairs are 800 px web exports. They reveal background shapes but cannot support camera-pixel-level examination.

SonyAlpha same-lens-model source captions were not enough to establish comparison suitability: four downloaded candidates are retained in excludedImages with the visual rejection reason and do not appear as A/B pairs.

Sony 85mm f/1.8 baseline versus original GM: three wide-open pairs and three near-aperture pairs found. The source labels the stopped-down GM f/1.7, so these do not fulfill an exact f/1.8 match. All are 800 px web exports.
