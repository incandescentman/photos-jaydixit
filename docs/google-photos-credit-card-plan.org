* Purpose and audience

This reusable image introduces Jay Dixit as the photographer to people browsing a shared Google Photos album. * Album plan and current status

Upload the square card with each shared Google Photos album so viewers see the permission and credit request before browsing. Keep the 4:5 version available as an alternative; no separate use for it has been selected.

Google Photos orders an album by capture date, and an image with no capture date lands at upload time, which is the end of the album. Upload the JPEGs, not the PNGs: Google Photos reads capture dates from JPEG EXIF reliably, and PNG metadata support is spotty. Both JPEGs carry EXIF ~DateTimeOriginal~ and ~CreateDate~ values of ~2000:01:01 00:00:00~, with ~Artist~ and ~Copyright~ set to ~Jay Dixit~ (verified with ExifTool, November 1, 2026). The early date is sorting metadata, not the portrait’s capture date; it puts the card before every photograph in an album ordered oldest first. Automatic placement has not yet been verified in Google Photos, so check after uploading and drag the card to the front if needed. The JPEGs are saved at quality 95 with no chroma subsampling, so the text stays crisp. The PNGs are the masters; if a card is ever re-rendered, regenerate the JPEGs from the new PNGs with the same date.

Both JPEGs and PNG masters are saved locally at the permanent paths above. No Google Photos album has been changed in this session. The plan is manual reuse; no automated upload or insertion has been implemented. Storage under the site’s public directory does not establish that the files have been committed, deployed, or published.
