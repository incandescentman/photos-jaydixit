The Red Carpet Mark

Fifteen radically simplified evocations of the red carpet — the site's own niche, distilled to a chunky wedge, a bold ribbon, a lone star, a flashbulb. Each is a 32×32 mark built to survive at 16px.

Warm-cream tile, red-carpet red, antique gold — the family grammar shared with jaydixit.com and storytelling.nyc. Every card shows the mark at 48px and at true 18px; the tab strips at the bottom are the money shot.
