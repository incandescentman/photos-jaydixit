Viewfinder framing a gold dot

Corner brackets — camera focus frame, crop marks — closing in on a single dot as “the subject.” The mark is a small argument about what this site does: photography as the deliberate act of framing.

Sixteen variants across bracket weight, dot treatment, color assignment, tile, and composition. Every mark is bold, geometric, and built to survive at 16px next to a family of siblings — the pink halo-dot of jaydixit.com, the navy sunburst of storytelling.nyc.
