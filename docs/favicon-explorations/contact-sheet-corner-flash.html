The Corner Flash Family

Twelve variations on the red-carpet wedge with a gold flash in the upper corner. This sheet tests whether the flash can become a real signal at 16px instead of a tiny decorative speck.

The useful tension is simple: make the flash large enough to read, but keep the mark from becoming generic sparkle clip art.
