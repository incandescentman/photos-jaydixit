* Recommendation

Make the ending a shorter, deliberately composed signature with an obvious next step. Keep the warm paper, editorial serif, red italic Photos, and asymmetry that belong to this site.

- Start with roughly half the current desktop height. The present near-screen-height section separates the wordmark from the links with an empty middle; bringing them together would make the composition feel connected.
- Put a smaller but still substantial Jay Dixit Photos wordmark on the left and a prominent red Contact link on the right, aligned as one composition. Keep the existing wording. Giving Contact priority is my proposal: someone who has finished looking through the photographs has a clear way to reach you.
- Put Red Carpet, Galleries, and Before & After in a quieter navigation row beneath, retaining all three destinations. Replace the current four equally weighted bordered cells with spacing and a single thin divider.
- Reduce the thick top rule to a hairline and bring the copyright into the same closing area. Let the typography provide the emphasis.

The tradeoff is less monumental branding at the bottom of the page. Preserve enough scale and asymmetry that the ending still belongs to the approved splashy editorial design. A prototype should compare this direction with the current section in the context of the last photographs, at 1728 by 1117, 3008 by 1692, and phone width.
