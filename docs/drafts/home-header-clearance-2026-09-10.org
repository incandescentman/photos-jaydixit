* Treatment

From 1501 pixels wide, header height follows available window height: ~clamp(48px, calc(12svh - 82px), 120px)~. The seal and wordmark scale with that row, while the navigation keeps its current type size. At 1728 by 1117, the row is about 52 pixels high and the seal is about 38 pixels square, with seven pixels of space above it. At 3008 by 1692, the original 120-pixel row, 72-pixel seal, and wordmark sizes return exactly. The hover underline also stays closer to the letters in the shallow header, so it clears the pins on short windows.

The tradeoff is smaller branding on shorter desktop windows. The photographs retain their positions, dimensions, overlap, and clothespins. The halo remains unchanged. Compact menus at 1500 pixels and below retain their existing treatment.

* Measured clearance

Clearance is measured from the bottom of the navigation text's bounding rectangle plus one pixel for the halo to the highest visible pin. This includes the font's descent space and is a conservative bound on the painted letters. The four requested widths use a window height of 1117 pixels.

- At 1536 pixels wide, clearance is 8.9 pixels.
- At 1728 pixels wide, clearance is 12.8 pixels.
- At 1900 pixels wide, clearance is 14.2 pixels.
- At 2048 pixels wide, clearance is 15.3 pixels.
- At 2049 pixels wide, clearance is 16.3 pixels.
- At 3008 by 1692, clearance is 18.0 pixels and the complete top 120 pixels match the prior header capture pixel for pixel.
- The shortest tested cases, 1501 by 900 and 1536 by 900, retain 6.3 and 6.6 pixels of clearance. Their hover underlines also clear the pins.
